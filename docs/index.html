01 Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Time Boxed iOS app, developed by Greg Adams, handles information. It also describes this policy website and information you choose to send when requesting support.

02 Data used by the app

Your daily plans

Time Boxed saves your priorities, brain-dump notes, time-block text, dates, and scheduled times in the app’s local storage so you can return to saved days. Your planner content is not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

Settings

The app stores preferences such as your planning hours, time-block interval, number of priorities, appearance, and default export destination on your device.

Pro purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases through the App Store. Time Boxed uses Apple’s StoreKit framework to verify purchase status and unlock or restore Pro features. The app does not send purchase records to a developer-operated server, and we do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide developers with sales and transaction reports through App Store Connect.

03 Calendar and Reminders

Exporting is optional and requires Pro. When you export a filled time block, Time Boxed asks for the relevant system permission and uses Apple’s EventKit framework to save the item.

Calendar

The app requests write-only Calendar access to create an event with the selected block’s text, date, start time, and end time. It does not request access to read your existing events.

Reminders

Apple’s permission for this feature is full Reminders access. Time Boxed uses that access to find a writable reminders list and create a reminder with the selected block’s text and due date and time. The app does not read the contents of your existing reminders or upload them to us.

Exported items may sync through iCloud or another account connected to your Calendar or Reminders app, according to your account settings and that provider’s privacy practices. People with access to shared calendars or lists may also see exported items.

04 Advertising and tracking

Time Boxed has no advertising, analytics SDK, third-party crash-reporting SDK, cross-app tracking, or developer-operated backend. It does not require an app account, collect advertising identifiers, or sell your planner content. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health data.

Apple may process App Store usage and diagnostic information under its own policies and your device settings.

05 Your choices and permissions

You can deny Calendar or Reminders access and continue planning in Time Boxed. You can change either permission later in your device’s Settings. Revoking permission stops future exports to that destination; it does not remove items you already exported.

You can manage or cancel an App Store subscription in your Apple account’s subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

06 Retention and deletion

Plans and preferences remain in the app’s local storage until you change or clear the relevant content or delete the app and its data. Offloading the app may retain its data. Device backups may also contain app data and are managed through your device and backup provider’s settings.

Deleting planner content or the app does not delete events or reminders you already exported. Manage those items in Calendar or Reminders. Apple manages purchase records under its own policies.

07 Platform services and this website

Time Boxed uses Apple services for purchases and optional exports. Apple’s handling of information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded trackers, or forms. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details when serving the page, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

08 Security

Time Boxed stores plans in the app’s private container and relies on Apple’s app sandbox and device protections. Protect your device and any backups or accounts used for exported items. No method of electronic storage is completely secure.

09 Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The date at the top of the page identifies the latest version.

10 Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Time Boxed GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public, so please do not include private plans, payment details, or other sensitive information. Information you choose to submit is used to respond to your request and remains in the issue thread unless removed.
